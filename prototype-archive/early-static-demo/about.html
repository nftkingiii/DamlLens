About The Dashboard

A Canton ecosystem observability layer.

This dashboard is designed for ecosystem teams, developers, validators, and application operators who need a clear view of Canton usage, settlement quality, participant concentration, and emerging business workflows.

What it measures

Activity volume, message throughput, participant count, app-level growth, finality quality, and flow concentration.

Who it helps

Builders can track adoption, validators can monitor operational health, and partners can identify pilot-ready workflows.

Why Canton

Canton is built for synchronized business applications, so ecosystem analytics should make cross-party activity understandable.
